How it works

Your timeline, edited for you

01 —

Point it at your song

Pick an audio track of the timeline, choose a song from your Media Pool (Pulse Studio inserts it for you), or import a file. Hit Detect Beats: AI beat detection maps every beat, bar and subdivision, and places the markers — on the timeline or on the audio clip itself.

Choose clips & rhythm

Use a curated Media Pool folder, or ★ Master to pull every clip you have — subfolders included. Order them sequentially, at random, or chronologically by shooting time (built for weddings). Pick a cut pattern, or let AI mood read the song and decide.

Clips land frame-exact on the beats, directly in your Resolve timeline. Animated punch-ins and speed ramps — slow-motion dives, speed-up whips — are eased, beat-aware, and rendered with Optical Flow retiming (Speed Warp on extreme slow-mo).
