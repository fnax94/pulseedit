How to auto-edit videos to music in DaVinci Resolve

Three steps. Zero manual cutting. Your edit synced perfectly to every beat.

1

Detect beats in your music

Select your audio track and click Detect. Pulse Edit uses AI-powered beat analysis to find every beat, bar, and subdivision. Adjust sensitivity to catch only strong beats or every nuance. Supports any time signature: 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 5/4, 6/4, 7/4.

2

Auto-edit clips to beats

Choose a cut pattern (Energy Map, Every Beat, Buildup, Wedding, and 7 more). Pulse Edit automatically takes clips from your Media Pool, cuts them to length, and places them on the timeline perfectly synced to every beat. Sequential or random clip order.

3

Adapt the edit with the AI mood analyzer

Choose a mood (Calm, Energetic, Epic...) and Pulse Edit Free analyzes your music and adapts the editing pattern — cut density and pacing — to match the song's energy. Every edit feels matched to the track.
