Questions

Quick answers

What is Pulse Color?

An OFX color-grading plugin for DaVinci Resolve — Free and Studio — that takes camera log footage to a finished film look in one node, with stackable emulations of on-set optical filters (Black Pro-Mist, halation, anamorphic streak). Included in the Pulse license together with Pulse Edit.

Does it work on DaVinci Resolve Free?

Yes. Pulse Color is a standard OpenFX plugin, and OFX works in Resolve Free as well as Studio (18.5+), on macOS and Windows. The macOS build is notarized by Apple; the Windows build is Authenticode-signed.

Is it a LUT pack?

No. Every camera profile is generated and verified against the colour-science reference library, the looks are full print-film rendering chains, and the filter FX are spatial effects no .cube file can contain — a LUT cannot do halation or Pro-Mist.

Is there a Black Pro-Mist plugin for Resolve?

Yes — Pulse Color emulates Black Pro-Mist, White Pro-Mist, Glimmerglass and Skin Diffusion as stackable OFX effects, with sliders graded like the physical filters (0.25 = a 1/4 filter). Works in Resolve Free and Studio.

How much does it cost?

Nothing extra: it's included in the Pulse license (€13/month or €130/year) together with Pulse Edit. One key, the whole suite, 14-day money-back guarantee, offline-friendly activation (30 days at a time).
