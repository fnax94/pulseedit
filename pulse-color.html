The looks

Six looks. Zero fake grain by default.

Nordic · Cinema Print · Midnight · Daylight · Linen · Amber — print-film rendering chains (cross-talk, highlight desaturation, palette shaping), not Instagram filters. A Look Strength dial blends each from subtle to full. Film character comes from curve, color and light — grain is there if you want it, off if you don't.

The filter drawer

Stackable, like real glass

Twelve optical FX, each with its own toggle, freely combinable — the way DPs stack filters on set.

A.

The mist family

Black Pro-Mist, White Pro-Mist, Glimmerglass, Skin Diffusion — sliders graded like the physical filters: 0.125 = 1/8, 0.25 = 1/4, 0.5 = 1/2, 1 = full.

B.

Light effects

Halation, Bloom, Anamorphic Streak and a 4/6/8-point Star Filter — measured to behave like the real thing.

C.

Finishing touches

Enhance, Chromatic Aberration, Vignette and Film Grain — all opt-in, all independent.

D.

LUT-accelerated engine on all CPU cores. Full-speed playback on a 25p HD timeline, no GPU requirements.
