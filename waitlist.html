What changed
01
No more slowed clips
When a clip was shorter than the cut it had to fill, DaVinci Resolve stretched it over the whole slot and it played slow — sometimes almost frozen. Pulse Edit now skips clips that are too short and picks one that actually fills the cut. Everything runs at 100% speed.
02
Weddings, in order
Chronological mode reads each clip's recording time and lays the day out as it happened. Two cameras interleave correctly when their clocks are in sync. Export one folder per part of the day for the best result.
03
Mixed frame rates
50fps footage on a 25fps timeline stays at 100% speed. You can also pin the timeline frame rate instead of letting it be guessed from the clips.
