For DaVinci Resolve Free

Pulse Edit

Built for everyone who uses the free version of DaVinci Resolve. Since November 2024, Blackmagic disabled the scripting API on DR Free, so plugins can't talk to Resolve directly anymore. Pulse Edit works around it: a standalone app analyzes your audio, builds the edit, and exports an OTIO timeline you import in Resolve with a double-click — beats, markers, cuts and clip order baked in. Works on DR Free and Studio.

  • Works on DR Free + Studio (OTIO universal import)
  • beat_this DNN beat detection (no drift)
  • 12 cut patterns + 6-mood + per-bar dynamic
  • Flow analysis: build-up & drop bursts
  • Bypasses BMD's Nov 2024 scripting restriction
  • 3 free generations to try — then pick a license
